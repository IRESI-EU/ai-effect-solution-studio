# AIE-38 participant brief — offline proposal review

**Prepared; awaiting human walkthrough.** No participant observations have been
recorded. Please use this brief with an observer.

This labelled offline fixture assesses understanding of a prepared proposal and
the review controls. It does not assess real-provider interpretation or the
complete first-time-user journey. The proposal is already prepared: you do not
need to type or send chat messages. Please do not use deployment controls.

The operator will sign in and open
[the prepared proposal](http://localhost:23122/design-ai?draftId=fe8110e0-631d-4527-aedc-2979e44ed83b).
Please use this copy only. You may open details and switch between the current
and proposed views. Think aloud where comfortable; uncertainty is useful feedback.

1. Before applying anything, explain in your own words what the proposal would
   change and what it would preserve. What would happen to the connections?
2. Select **Apply changes**, then **Undo**. Explain what you believe changed
   after each action.
3. Pause while the observer restores the same prepared fixture proposal, then
   refresh the page. This is a planned fixture setup step, not a chat task.
   Select **Apply changes** again, then **Save draft** and **Open in Build Solution**.
   Explain whether Builder shows the design you expected.
4. Based on what you saw, has an experiment executed? What on screen supports
   your answer?
5. Tell the observer about any wording or controls you found confusing. Use your
   own words; you do not need to choose new terminology.

You can ask for help or stop. The observer will record any assistance, including
the planned fixture setup. There is no participant performance score to optimize.
Existing product terms remain unchanged during this review.
