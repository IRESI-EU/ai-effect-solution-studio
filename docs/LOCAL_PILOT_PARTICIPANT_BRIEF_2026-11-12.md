# Supervised local Design with AI pilot

For two or three first-time users, one person at a time. The supervisor provides
a privately authenticated session using one existing reviewer account. This is
single-account usability testing, not a test of separate user permissions.

**Your task**

“Use the assistant to design a sample electricity-grid experiment that generates
a grid and benchmarks it. Choose seed 43, review the proposed design, save it,
and explain what you believe has happened.”

Work as you normally would. Say what you are thinking, especially when something
is unclear. You may request help; the observer will record assistance. Start
with a maximum of two Sends. Every Send, including a clarification, consumes
the same shared pilot allowance. Do not retry an error automatically.

Afterward, explain the two steps, their connection and settings, what Apply and
Save did, and whether you think anything executed. Unsupported requests,
questions and errors are useful findings; there is no need to obtain a pass.

This is a restricted local design-only pilot. No workflow execution is enabled.
